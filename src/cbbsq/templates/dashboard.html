What the columns mean
  • SQ proj.: ShotQuality's pregame projection turned into a total for this game.
  • Luck edge: how many points per 100 possessions these two teams' games have scored above (Under) or below (Over) what the shots were worth this season, both ends counted. Bigger = stronger spot.
  • From opp. shooting: how much of that edge comes from opponents' shooting. Blue (positive) agrees with the bet.
  • Last 5: the same edge over each team's last 5 games. Blue means recent games agree.
  • SQ edge: how many points ShotQuality's projected total is on the bet's side of the line. Blue means it agrees.

What the columns mean
  • Luck edge: how many more points per 100 possessions the team you're betting against has gained from luck this season (points scored and allowed vs. what the shots were worth). Bigger = stronger spot.
  • From opp. misses: how much of that edge comes from opponents missing good shots, which is mostly chance. Blue (positive) is better.
  • Last 5: the same edge over the last 5 games. Blue means recent games agree.
  • SQ edge: how many points ShotQuality's pregame projection likes the same bet by against the line. Blue means it agrees with the bet.

How to use it
  • Set the win % you want. The first table shows, for each number, the lowest value that has hit it. Click a row to add it to the filter; the Today tab then ticks the games that qualify.
  • The "≥" boxes stack, so you can test combinations such as Luck edge ≥ 4 and Last 5 ≥ 0. Everything on this tab then looks only at picks that pass the filter.
  • SQ edge: how many points ShotQuality's pregame projection likes the same bet by against the line. Negative means the projection prefers the other side.
  • SQ edge: how many points ShotQuality's projected total is on the bet's side of the line. Negative means the projection prefers the other side.
  • ±: the 95% range around the win %. A threshold found by looking back at results flatters itself, so trust it once the low end clears 52.4%, and check that it holds on new games.
